How many edges can a simple graph have?
Lemma
If G is a simple undirected graph with n vertices and m edges, then m ≤ n(n − 1)/2.
Proof
  1. No self-loops and no parallel edges: v joins each of the other n − 1 vertices at most once, so deg(v) ≤ n − 1.
  2. Add this up over all n vertices and use the handshaking lemma:
    2m = ∑v∈V deg(v) ≤ n(n − 1)
  3. Divide by 2: m ≤ n(n − 1)/2.
  • The maximum is Θ(n2): a complete graph has n(n − 1)/2 edges.
