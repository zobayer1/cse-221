How many edges can a simple directed graph have?
Lemma
If G is a simple directed graph with n vertices and m edges, then m ≤ n(n − 1).
Proof
  1. No self-loops and no parallel edges: v has at most one edge to each of the other n − 1 vertices, so outdeg(v) ≤ n − 1.
  2. Add this up over all n vertices and use the directed handshaking lemma:
    m = ∑v∈V outdeg(v) ≤ n(n − 1)
  • The maximum is Θ(n2): both ways on every pair give n(n − 1) edges.
