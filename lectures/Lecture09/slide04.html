Vertices and edges
Vertex B, its incident edges and its neighbors
  • Vertex or node: one object in the graph.
  • Edge: a link between two vertices, called its endpoints.
  • Adjacent: joined by an edge. B and D are adjacent.
  • Neighbors of B: its adjacent vertices, A, C, D.
  • Incident: an edge is incident on its endpoints. Three edges are incident on B.
