Comparing the three representations
RepresentationSpaceTraversal
Neighbors of uWhole graph
Adjacency matrixΘ(n2)Θ(n)Θ(n2)
Adjacency listΘ(n + m)Θ(deg(u))Θ(n + m)
Edge listΘ(n + m)Θ(deg(u))Θ(n + m)
  • Dense graph, m = Θ(n2): all three take Θ(n2) space and whole-graph time.
  • Sparse graph, m much smaller than n2: the two lists are far cheaper.
  • Directed graph: the same bounds, with outdeg(u) in place of deg(u).
