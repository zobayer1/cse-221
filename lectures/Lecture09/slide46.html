Can BFS find cycles?
Undirected graph: yes. While scanning Adj[u], if a neighbor v is not white and v ≠ u.π, then edge (u, v) and the tree path between u and v form a cycle.

Undirected

Scanning a: b is gray, b ≠ a.π. A cycle.

Directed

Scanning a: b is gray, b ≠ a.π. No cycle.

Directed graph: not with this test. A non-white neighbor may just have a second path into it.
