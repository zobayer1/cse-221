Trees: any two imply the third
Lemma
For an undirected graph G with n vertices, any two of the following imply the third:
  1. G is connected.
  2. G does not contain a cycle.
  3. G has n − 1 edges.
1 + 2 ⇒ 3
1 + 3 ⇒ 2
2 + 3 ⇒ 1
All three hold for a tree:
n = 7, m = 6

Every tree on n vertices has n − 1 edges. To recognize a tree, check any two.
