Minimum hops from s to t
t.d = 2, so the minimum is 2 hops. Follow π back: t, w, s.
Definition
The shortest-path distance δ(s, v) is the minimum number of edges on any path from s to v, or ∞ if there is none.
Theorem (CLRS 22.5)
When BFS finishes, v.d = δ(s, v) for every vertex v.

The blue and red π edges form the breadth-first tree, rooted at s.
Hops count edges only: on a weighted graph, fewest hops need not be cheapest.
