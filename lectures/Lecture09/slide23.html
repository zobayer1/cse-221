Exam question
An undirected graph G with n vertices is connected and has n − 1 edges. Prove that G is a tree.
Proof
  1. Given: G is connected and has n − 1 edges.
  2. By the lemma (slide 22), connected and n − 1 edges imply no cycle.
  3. All three properties hold. G is connected and has no cycles, so G is a tree.
  • Any two will do. Given no cycle and n − 1 edges, the lemma gives connected the same way.
