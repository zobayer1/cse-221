Edge list construction

Starts with count = 0 and head[u] = NIL for every u.

Puts the new edge at the front of u’s chain: Θ(1) time.

Follows u’s chain until NIL: Θ(deg(u)) time.

An undirected edge {u, v} takes two calls: ADD-EDGE(u, v) and ADD-EDGE(v, u).
