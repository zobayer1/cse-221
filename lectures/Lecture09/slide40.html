BFS: time complexity
WorkLinesWhyCost
Initialization2–10Touches every vertex onceO(V)
Queue work12, 18, 19Only a white vertex enters Q, so each vertex enters at most onceO(V)
Scanning lists13–17Each list is scanned at most once. By the handshaking lemma, the lists hold 2m entries (m if directed)O(E)
TotalO(V + E)
  • Inside O, V means |V| = n and E means |E| = m.
  • The complexity depends on the graph representation. This bound holds for adjacency lists and the edge list. With an adjacency matrix, scanning alone costs O(V2).
