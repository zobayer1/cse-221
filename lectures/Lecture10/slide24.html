The substitution method

A recursion tree gives a good guess, but we were sloppy: n a power of 2, floors dropped, “⋯” in the middle.
To be sure, we prove the guess.

1

Guess the form of the answer

For example T(n) ≤ cn log n. Guesses come from a recursion tree, from unrolling, from a recurrence you have seen before, or from a table of small values.

2

Prove it by induction

Assume the guess for smaller inputs and substitute it into the recurrence. Then find the constants c and n0 that make it work, as in Lecture 6.

The guess is substituted for T on smaller inputs: hence the name (CLRS §4.3).
