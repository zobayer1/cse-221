T(n) = T(n − 1) + T(n − 2) + c

At most

Use T(n − 2) ≤ T(n − 1): T(n) ≤ 2T(n − 1) + c, HANOI’s recurrence, so O(2n).

At least

Use T(n − 1) ≥ T(n − 2): T(n) ≥ 2T(n − 2). Doubling every two steps gives Ω(2n/2) ≈ Ω(1.41n).

Exponential either way. (The exact rate is Θ(1.618n), the golden ratio.) FIB(n − 2) is computed again inside FIB(n − 1): overlapping subproblems (Lecture 8).
