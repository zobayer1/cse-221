Types of edges
TypeEdge (u, v) goes tov when (u, v) is first explored
Treea child: exploring (u, v) discovered vWHITE
Backan ancestor of u, or u itself (a self-loop)GRAY
Forwarda descendant of u, but not a childBLACK, and u.d < v.d
Crossany other vertex: neither an ancestor nor a descendantBLACK, and u.d > v.d

Why gray means back: the gray vertices are the path from the root to u.
