DFS: time complexity
WorkLinesWhyCost
Initialization, main loopDFS2–8Touches every vertex twice, not counting the callsO(V)
The callsDFS-VISIT2–4, 9–11Only a white vertex gets a call, which paints it gray at once: one call per vertexO(V)
Scanning listsDFS-VISIT5–8Each list is scanned once, in its vertex’s call. The lists hold 2m entries (m if directed)O(E)
TotalO(V + E)
  • DFS visits every vertex and scans every list, so the bound is tight: Θ(V + E).
  • The complexity depends on the graph representation. With an adjacency matrix, scanning alone costs O(V2).
