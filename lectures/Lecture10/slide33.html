The master theorem
Master theorem (for f(n) = Θ(nk))

Let a ≥ 1, b > 1 and k ≥ 0 be constants, and T(n) = aT(n/b) + Θ(nk). Then:

CaseCompareSolutionWho wins
1a > bkΘ(nlogb a)the leaves
2a = bkΘ(nk log n)every level
3a < bkΘ(nk)the root

Compare a (how fast the calls multiply) with bk (how fast the work per call shrinks).
Same thing with logs: compare k with logb a. The cases are numbered as in CLRS.
