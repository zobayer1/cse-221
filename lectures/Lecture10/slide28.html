Why a back edge means a cycle
Lemma 22.11 (CLRS)
A directed graph has a cycle if and only if DFS finds a back edge.
Back edge ⇒ cycle
Let (u, v) be a back edge. Then v is an ancestor of u, so the tree has a path from v to u. The edge (u, v) closes it into a cycle.
Cycle ⇒ back edge
Let v be the first vertex of the cycle that DFS discovers, and u the vertex just before v on the cycle. When v turns gray, the rest of the cycle is white and reachable from v, so DFS reaches u before v finishes. When it explores (u, v), v is still gray: a back edge.
