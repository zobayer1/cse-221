Induction in one slide
Base case
Show the claim for the smallest size, such as n = 0 or n = 2.
Inductive step
Assume the claim for every size smaller than n. Show it for n.

Then the claim holds for every size. With T(n/2) in the recurrence, the step uses size n/2, not n − 1:
that is why we assume every smaller size (strong induction).
