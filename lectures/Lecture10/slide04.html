Breadth-first search: the idea
Breadth-first search (BFS)
Starting from the source s, BFS discovers the vertices layer by layer:
L0 = {s}
Lk+1 = { v : (u, v) ∈ E for some u ∈ Lk, and v is in no earlier layer }

All of Lk is discovered before any of Lk+1.
Lk holds the vertices at distance k from s: k edges away, and no fewer.
