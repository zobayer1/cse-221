An uneven split

One third and two thirds:T(n) = T(n/3) + T(2n/3) + cn

1

The sizes on a full level add up to n, so each full level costs cn.

2

Lower bound. All levels down to depth log3 n are full: T(n) ≥ cn log3 n.

3

Upper bound. No level costs more than cn, and there are at most log3/2 n + 1 levels: T(n) ≤ cn(log3/2 n + 1).

4

Both are n log n times a constant (the base of a log does not matter, Lecture 6): Θ(n log n).

This is why a 1 : 9 split every time still gives quicksort O(n log n) (Lecture 4).
