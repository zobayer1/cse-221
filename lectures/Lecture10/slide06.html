BFS: pseudocode
1BFS(G, s)
2    for each vertex u ∈ G.V − {s}
3        u.color = WHITE
4        u.d = ∞
5        u.π = NIL
6    s.color = GRAY
7    s.d = 0
8    s.π = NIL
9    Q = ∅
10    ENQUEUE(Q, s)
11    while Q ≠ ∅
12        u = DEQUEUE(Q)
13        for each v ∈ G.Adj[u]
14            if v.color == WHITE
15                v.color = GRAY
16                v.d = u.d + 1
17                v.π = u
18                ENQUEUE(Q, v)
19        u.color = BLACK
WHITE: not discovered yet
GRAY: discovered, waiting in Q (the frontier)
BLACK: all its neighbors have been scanned
  • v.d: distance from s, ∞ until discovered
  • v.π: predecessor, the vertex that discovered v
  • Q: a first-in, first-out queue of gray vertices
  • G.Adj[u]: the adjacency list of u
