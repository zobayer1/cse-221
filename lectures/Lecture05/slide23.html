read the finished buckets left to right: ACE BAD BAT BED CAB CAT

bucket of one word: doneA letter being sorted on
1

Sort on the first letter into buckets.

2

Sort each bucket on its own on the next letter, by recursion or with a stack. Later passes only break ties inside a bucket.

3

Stop as soon as a bucket holds one key: ACE and BED never need their later letters.

Natural for lexicographic order: words, strings, keys of different lengths. The price: keeping track of many small buckets.

Caution
A pass over the whole list that moves nothing does not mean we can stop: [21, 12] is unchanged by the ones pass, yet unsorted. Only a bucket can stop early.
