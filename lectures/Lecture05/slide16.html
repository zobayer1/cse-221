Why “radix” sort?

Radix is Latin for “root”. In math it is the base of a number system.

hundreds
2digit 1

Base 10: every digit is one of 10 values. Base 2 has 2 (bits), base 256 has 256 (bytes).

Before computers

Punched-card sorters did radix sort by machine: one column per pass, then stack the bins in order (CLRS §8.3).
