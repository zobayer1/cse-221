Digits

Digit 0 is the ones digit, digit d − 1 the highest.
Digit i of x is ⌊x / 10i⌋ mod 10.

The stable sort

Counting sort with keys 0 to 9 (next slide): count and place each element by its digit i, but copy the whole number into B.
