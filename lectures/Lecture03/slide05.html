Bubble sort
1BUBBLE-SORT(A, n)
2    for i = 0 to n − 2
3        for j = 0 to n − 2 − i
4            if A[j] > A[j + 1]
5                exchange A[j] with A[j + 1]
What “swap” means: 3 moves, with a spare
Idea
  • Walk through the array and swap neighbors that are out of order.
  • After pass 1, the largest element has bubbled to the end. After pass k, the last k are in place.
  • Always makes n − 1 passes, even if the array becomes sorted sooner.
