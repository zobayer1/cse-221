Insertion sort
1INSERTION-SORT(A, n)
2    for i = 1 to n − 1
3        key = A[i]
4        j = i − 1
5        while j ≥ 0 and A[j] > key
6            A[j + 1] = A[j]
7            j = j − 1
8        A[j + 1] = key
Idea
  • The left part A[0..i − 1] is sorted. Take the next element, the key.
  • Shift larger elements one step right, then drop the key into the gap.
  • It moves elements by shifting, not swapping. One shift is one write.
