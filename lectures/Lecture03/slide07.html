Both loops run in full whatever the input, so bubble sort always makes n(n − 1)/2 comparisons, even on a sorted array. Only the number of swaps changes.
Worst case: every pair is out of order, so every comparison causes a swap: (n − 1) + (n − 2) + … + 1 = n(n − 1)/2.
