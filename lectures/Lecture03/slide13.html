Selection sort
1SELECTION-SORT(A, n)
2    for i = 0 to n − 2
3        min = i
4        for j = i + 1 to n − 1
5            if A[j] < A[min]
6                min = j
7        if min ≠ i
8            exchange A[i] with A[min]
Idea
  • Scan the unsorted part A[i..n − 1] to find the smallest element.
  • Swap it into position i. Now A[0..i] holds the i + 1 smallest, in their final places.
  • At most one swap per pass, however the input looks.
