Telling them apart

Bubble sort

Bubbles rise to the top
One passSwaps neighbors all the way along
Sorted partGrows from the right, final
Best caseStill O(n2): always compares
MovesMany swaps: 13 in our example

Insertion sort

Sorting cards in your hand
One passTakes the next element and slides it into the sorted part
Sorted partGrows from the left, not final
Best caseO(n) on sorted input
MovesMany shifts: 13 in our example

Selection sort

Pick the smallest, put it next
One passSearches the unsorted part for the minimum
Sorted partGrows from the left, final
Best caseStill O(n2): always scans
MovesAt most n − 1 swaps: 5 in our example
Insertion vs selection: selection searches the unsorted part for what comes next. Insertion takes what comes next and searches the sorted part for where it goes.
Same 13? Bubble swaps and insertion shifts both equal the number of inversions: pairs that are out of order. Each one fixes exactly one.
