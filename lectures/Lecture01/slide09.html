Order of growth

The order of growth of a running time is its leading term, without the constant factor.

  • It describes how the algorithm behaves for large n: its asymptotic behavior.
  • Lower-order terms and constant factors stop mattering as n grows.
From the MIN exampleT(n) = 3n − 1  →  order of growth n
Why only the leading term?
