A first look at divide and conquer

Divide

Split the problem into smaller parts.Binary search: split the range at the middle.

Conquer

Solve the smaller parts, usually the same way.Binary search: search only the half that can contain x.

Combine

Join the partial answers into one.Binary search: nothing to do, the half's answer is the answer.

Binary search keeps only one of the two halves, so it is sometimes called decrease and conquer. We will study divide and conquer properly later, with merge sort and quicksort.
