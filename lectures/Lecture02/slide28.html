Is A[i] < A[i + 1]?  Y Y Y Y Y N N N flips once: a monotonic yes/no test. The peak is at the first N.

1PEAK(A, n)
2    lo = 0
3    hi = n − 1
4    while lo < hi
5        mid = lo + ⌊(hi − lo) / 2⌋
6        if A[mid] < A[mid + 1]
7            lo = mid + 1
8        else
9            hi = mid
10    return lo

Here lo < hi and hi = mid are correct: the invariant is “the peak is in [lo, hi]”, and mid + 1 never leaves it. Conventions must match the invariant.
