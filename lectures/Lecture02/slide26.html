Use 2: finding the peak

An optimization problem: on a range [lo, hi], find the x where f(x) is largest (or smallest). Ternary search can do this when f is unimodal.

Unimodal: one maximum

Strictly rises, then strictly falls. Exactly one peak.

Unimodal: one minimum

Falls, then rises. The same idea finds the bottom of the valley.

Not unimodal

Several local maxima. Ternary search may stop at the wrong peak.

Examples: the ticket price that brings in the most money, the launch angle that throws a ball farthest, or the point on a path closest to a target.
