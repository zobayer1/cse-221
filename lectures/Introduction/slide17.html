What is an algorithm?

An algorithm is a finite sequence of well-defined instructions that takes an input and produces the correct output for a given problem, in a finite amount of time.

Finite

A limited number of steps, and it always stops.

Well-defined

Each step is precise and unambiguous, in a fixed order.

Correct

Right output for every valid input, not just some.

Where the word comes from

Muhammad ibn Musa al‑Khwarizmi

Persian mathematician, c. 780–850, House of Wisdom, Baghdad

His book on calculating with Hindu–Arabic numerals taught step-by-step methods for arithmetic. Medieval Latin translations wrote his name as Algoritmi.

His book al-Jabr also gave us the word algebra.

al‑Khwarizmi → Algoritmi → algorithm
