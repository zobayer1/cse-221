Algorithm specification

Every algorithm must have these five properties

1

Input

Takes zero or more inputs from outside the algorithm.

2

Output

Produces at least one output as its result.

3

Definiteness

Every step is clear and unambiguous. It can be read only one way.

4

Finiteness

Stops after a finite number of steps, for every valid input.

5

Effectiveness

Every step is basic enough to actually carry out. In principle, by hand with pen and paper.

These five properties follow Donald Knuth, The Art of Computer Programming, Vol. 1.
