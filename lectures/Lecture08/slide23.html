Three possible cases

The array is split at mid = 3: left half A[0..3], right half A[4..7].

1Entirely in the left half

j ≤ mid: search the left half only.

RMQ(0, 2) = 3

2Entirely in the right half

i > mid: search the right half only.

RMQ(5, 7) = 2

3Split between the two halves

i ≤ mid < j: find the minimum of each piece, return the smaller.

RMQ(1, 5) = min(RMQ(1, 3), RMQ(4, 5)) = min(3, 2) = 2
