9    ac = MULTIPLY(a, c, n − k)
10    ad = MULTIPLY(a, d, n − k)

12    bd = MULTIPLY(b, d, n − k)

The steps
Base caseLines 2–3: one digit times one digit, Θ(1).
DivideLines 4–8 split off the last k digits: Θ(n) digit copies.
ConquerLines 9–12: four products of numbers with at most n − k = ⌈n/2⌉ digits.
CombineLine 13: multiplying by 102k or 10k only pads zeros, so the shifts and sums are Θ(n).
