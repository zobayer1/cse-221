FIND-MAX-CROSSING-SUBARRAY
1FIND-MAX-CROSSING-SUBARRAY(A, lo, mid, hi)
2    left-sum = −∞
3    sum = 0
4    for i = mid downto lo
5        sum = sum + A[i]
6        if sum > left-sum
7            left-sum = sum
8            max-left = i
9    right-sum = −∞
10    sum = 0
11    for j = mid + 1 to hi
12        sum = sum + A[j]
13        if sum > right-sum
14            right-sum = sum
15            max-right = j
16    return (max-left, max-right, left-sum + right-sum)
How it works
Lines 2–8Left piece: from mid down to lo, keep the best sum of A[i..mid].
Lines 9–15Right piece: from mid + 1 up to hi, keep the best sum of A[mid + 1..j].
Line 16Glue the two pieces together.
Why −∞?The first sum always wins, so each piece takes at least one element.
Cost(mid − lo + 1) + (hi − mid) = n iterations: Θ(n).
