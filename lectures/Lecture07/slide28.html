Worth the effort

Divide and conquer is far faster than brute force. It needed one clever step, the crossing scan, and a little more code, but the payoff is large.

One technique of several

Divide and conquer is one of several powerful ways to design algorithms. A closer look at a problem can still beat it, as the scan does here.

Analyzed with recurrences

Its running time is a recurrence, here T(n) = 2T(n/2) + Θ(n), and it often leads to faster algorithms.
