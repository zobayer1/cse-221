What we learned today
Takeaways
1

Divide, conquer, combine

Split a problem into smaller copies, solve them recursively, and join their answers. The base cases have constant size.

2

Cost as a recurrence

T(n) = a · T(n/b) + D(n) + C(n). For the maximum subarray, 2T(n/2) + Θ(n) = Θ(n log n).

3

Left, right or crossing

The best subarray lies in one half or crosses the middle. The crossing one grows out from the middle in Θ(n).

4

Four ways, four speeds

Brute force Θ(n3), running sum Θ(n2), divide and conquer Θ(n log n), and one scan that drops negative stretches, Θ(n).
