Even faster: one observation

Sum ≥ 0: keep going

Scan left to right with a running sum. A stretch with a sum of at least 0 can only help what comes after it.

Sum < 0: drop it, restart

Everything up to and including the item that made the sum negative can never be part of a larger sum. Start again at the next item.

Why: if A[s..i] sums below 0, then for every j > i, sum of A[s..j] < sum of A[i + 1..j].
