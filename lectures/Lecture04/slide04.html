Stable sorting

A sort is stable if elements with equal keys keep their original order.

Input, sort by marks

Rafi80
Mitu70
Joy80
Tania70

Stable

Mitu70
Tania70
Rafi80
Joy80

Not stable

Tania70
Mitu70
Rafi80
Joy80

Why care? Sort by name, then stably by marks: students with equal marks stay in name order.

Only equal keys matter. Rafi and Joy may never swap order, but Mitu may move ahead of Rafi: their keys differ.
