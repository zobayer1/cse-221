Attendance
Attendance carries no marks, but meeting both requirements below is mandatory to sit for the final exam.
70%

Theory

Recorded by your theory instructor
  • Absences with a valid cause are not credited right away.
  • They are considered at the end of the course, only if you fall short of 70% before the final.
90%

Lab

Recorded by your lab instructor
  • Your theory instructor has no say in lab attendance.
  • Sort out any lab absence directly with your lab instructor.

These requirements are set by the course committee.
