A simple example: guess my number

I am thinking of a number between 1 and 1,000.

You guess, and I answer higher, lower or correct.

Question
What is the fewest guesses that always works, however unlucky you are?
NumbersOne by oneGuess the middle
1,0001,00010
1,000,0001,000,00020
1,000,000,0001,000,000,00030

Worst-case number of guesses. Guessing the middle halves what is left each time: that is binary search, and it needs about log2 n guesses.
