Big-Theta in a picture
Read the picture
Red is the ceiling c2 · g(n), green the floor c1 · g(n): the same g, two multipliers.
Left of n0, f(n) may leave the band, above or below.
From n0 on, f(n) stays inside the band, forever.

So f(n) = Θ(g(n)): a tight bound.
