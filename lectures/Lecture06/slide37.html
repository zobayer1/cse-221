A common mix-up: “O is the worst case, Ω is the best case.” No: best and worst case are two different functions, and each one gets its own bounds.

Worst case: Θ(n). Best case: Θ(1).
On every input, the running time is O(n) (the worst case caps it) and Ω(1).
Back to slide 3: is quicksort O(n2) or O(n log n)?
Its worst case is Θ(n2) and its best and average cases are Θ(n log n). So “quicksort is O(n2)” holds on every input, while O(n log n) holds only for its best and average cases. A bound on the worst case is a bound on every input (CLRS, p. 48).
