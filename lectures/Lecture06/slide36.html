Count the runs
Now the inner loop runs k times: 1 + 2 + 4 + … + 2m, where 2m is the largest power of 2 that is ≤ n.
That sum is 2m + 1 − 1, which lies between n and 2n.

COUNT-C is Θ(n)not Θ(n log n)!
