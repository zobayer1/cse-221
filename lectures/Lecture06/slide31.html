Three facts behind the ladder
Any power of log n grows slower than any power of n: logb n = o(na) for constants a > 0, b.
CLRS, p. 57
Any power of n grows slower than any exponential: nb = o(an) for constants a > 1, b.
CLRS eq. 3.10, p. 55
n! grows faster still: n! = ω(2n).
CLRS eq. 3.19, p. 58
