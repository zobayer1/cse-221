Why topological sort works
Key fact (CLRS Theorem 22.12)
In a DAG, for every edge (u, v): v.f < u.f. So listing by decreasing f puts u before v.

When DFS explores (u, v), u is gray. What color can v be?

Gray? Then (u, v) is a back edge, so G has a cycle. Impossible in a DAG.
White? Then v becomes a descendant of u, and finishes first: v.f < u.f.
Black? Then v has already finished, and u has not: v.f < u.f.
