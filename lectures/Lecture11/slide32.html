The full master theorem
Theorem 4.1 in CLRS

Let a ≥ 1 and b > 1 be constants and T(n) = aT(n/b) + f(n). Compare f(n) with nlogb a:

1If f(n) = O(nlogb a − ε) for some constant ε > 0, then T(n) = Θ(nlogb a).
2If f(n) = Θ(nlogb a), then T(n) = Θ(nlogb a log n).
3If f(n) = Ω(nlogb a + ε) for some constant ε > 0, and a f(n/b) ≤ c f(n) for some constant c < 1 and all large n, then T(n) = Θ(f(n)).

Same idea

The larger of f(n) (the root) and nlogb a (the leaves) wins. A tie adds a log.

Our version is a special case

With f(n) = Θ(nk), take ε = |logb a − k|. In case 3, a(n/b)k = (a/bk) nk with a/bk < 1.

Bigger by a power of n

Cases 1 and 3 need f(n) to differ from nlogb a by a factor nε. A factor of only log n is not enough.
