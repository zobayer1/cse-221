DFS: colors and data
Each vertex u keeps
  • u.color: WHITE, GRAY or BLACK
  • u.π: predecessor, the vertex that discovered u
  • u.d: discovery time, when u turns gray
  • u.f: finish time, when u turns black
Colors
WHITE: not discovered yet
GRAY: discovered at u.d, still exploring its edges
BLACK: finished at u.f, every edge explored

Careful: in BFS, v.d was a distance. In DFS, u.d is a time.
