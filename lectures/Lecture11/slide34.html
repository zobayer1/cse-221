Topological sort: the full procedure
1TOPOLOGICAL-SORT(G)
2    let L be a new empty linked list
3    DFS(G)
4    return L
  • Line 12 of DFS-VISIT is the only new step: right after u.f = time, u goes to the front of L.
  • So L holds the vertices in decreasing order of f: the last vertex to finish comes first.
  • Why? A vertex finishes only after everything it leads to has finished, so everything that must come after it is already in L.
  • Time: Θ(V + E), for DFS plus an O(1) insertion per vertex.
