Three kinds of sums

A geometric sum: each term is r times the one before.1 + r + r2 + ⋯ + rk

Shrinking: r < 1

1 + ½ + ¼ + ⋯ < 2

The first term wins

The sum is less than 1/(1 − r), a constant.

Level: r = 1

1 + 1 + ⋯ + 1 = k + 1

Every term counts

One term × the number of terms.

Growing: r > 1

1 + 2 + ⋯ + 2k < 2 · 2k

The last term wins

The sum is less than r/(r − 1) × the last term.

For r ≠ 1: 1 + r + ⋯ + rk = rk+1 − 1r − 1CLRS (A.5)
In a recursion tree, the level totals often form this sum: find r, and you know which level wins.
